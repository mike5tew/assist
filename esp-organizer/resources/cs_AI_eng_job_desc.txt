About the job
Job summary
The Integrated Corporate Services (ICS) is a new shared corporate service, launched in July 2023. It provides corporate services (HR, Finance, Digital, Commercial, Security and Estates) across the Department for Energy Security & Net Zero (DESNZ) and the Department for Science, Innovation & Technology (DSIT).

Our team of just over 300 professionals will be leading the way in how these functions will be delivered in the future. Our ambition is to be the leading provider of integrated corporate services for government and set the standard for quality, efficiency, and innovation in our field.

We offer great working benefits including a world-class pension, flexible working options and a career where your learning and development is taken seriously. We are enormously proud to be a Disability Confident Leader employer. We support candidates with adjustments throughout our recruitment process. Information about disability confidence and just some examples of the adjustments that you can request can be found in the reasonable adjustment section below.

Job description
Join ICS Digital, where innovation meets expertise to create digital solutions. We are a cohesive digital community who work in the open, solving problems together and fostering a culture of transparency and collaboration. Our commitment to user-focused design and data-driven decision-making ensures that we deliver accessible services tailored to the needs of our customers.

The Data & Development team sits within ICS Digital, focusing on managing and evolving our internal platforms such as the Data Management System, Salesforce and Azure as well as related digital services. We are now branching out to integrate advanced AI and automation into these solutions to find efficiencies.

As one of two Applied AI Engineers, you will spearhead the use of AI in our products – designing intelligent features, automating complex processes, and bringing cutting-edge machine learning capabilities into our internal platforms. This role is pivotal to transforming how we deliver corporate services across DESNZ and DSIT by leveraging AI to simplify the mundane and drive innovation.

Visit our blog to learn more about us and our work: https://icsdigital.blog.gov.uk/
Person specification
•  Lead development of AI solutions: Architect, implement, and deploy AI-driven features within the Data Management System, Salesforce and Azure as well as related digital services. Ensuring they are production-ready, scalable, and maintainable. You will own end-to-end delivery of AI components, from prototypes to live services.

•  Innovate and prototype at pace: Rapidly experiment with new AI tools and models (for example, building generative AI agents or other ML-driven automation) to solve business problems. You’ll evaluate emerging technologies and integrate the best solutions quickly to continuously improve our services.

•  Embed AI-driven productivity: Champion the use of AI for developer productivity and operational efficiency. For instance, integrate AI-assisted coding, testing, or data analysis tools into our team’s workflow to accelerate development cycles and improve quality.

•  Stay at the cutting edge: Keep the team up-to-date with the latest in AI advancements. This includes exploring and applying leading-edge techniques (e.g. utilising large language models, vector databases, or emerging standards like the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for AI-tool integration) to ensure our solutions remain innovative and effective.

•  Collaborate across disciplines: Work closely with product managers, data engineers, analysts, and other developers to identify opportunities where AI can add value. Translate functional requirements into technical solutions,  and integrate AI components seamlessly into existing systems and workflows.

Mentor and set best practices: Provide technical leadership in AI engineering within the team.
Share your expertise, mentor junior engineers, and promote best practices in coding, model development, and MLOps (e.g. code review standards, model evaluation and monitoring, data ethics).
Foster a culture of continuous learning and responsible AI use in line with ICS Digital’s values

The above responsibilities are not exhaustive, and the post holders may be required to undertake some additional tasks not listed above, within the remit of their grade, to support the team.

As a line manager, you will be responsible for working with your members of staff to define their objectives, as well as managing their development and performance. 

Essential Criteria 

• Proven AI delivery – A track record of delivering AI-powered systems into production multiple times. Candidates should have built or led the implementation of complex AI solutions end-to-end on at least four occasions, demonstrating the ability to go from idea to live deployment (e.g. deploying a machine learning model or an AI agent as part of a real product).

• Deep technical expertise – Strong software engineering and machine learning skills. This includes proficiency in programming (such as Python Typescript etc or lower level such as Go, C#, Rust etc) and solid understanding of system design. You should be comfortable with cloud services and infrastructure used for AI (e.g. deploying models on AWS/Azure, using containers and CI/CD) to ensure solutions are robust and scalable.

• Modern AI toolkit proficiency – Hands-on experience with state-of-the-art AI technologies and frameworks. For example, you have built applications using large language models or other advanced ML models, implemented AI agents or conversational interfaces, or worked with cutting-edge toolsets (such as leveraging vector databases for semantic search, or integrating with MCP servers for AI-to-tool communication).

• Fast learner & proactive innovator – Demonstrated ability to quickly learn and adopt new tools or techniques. We are looking for someone who stays ahead of the curve: perhaps you have a history of trying out new open-source projects, participating in hackathons, or rapidly prototyping solutions with emerging AI APIs. Evidence of initiating improvements (automating a manual process, testing a new library, etc.) in your past work is highly valued.

• Leadership and communication – Strong ability to lead technical work and collaborate with others. You can break down complex AI concepts and communicate them clearly to non-experts (e.g. explaining model outputs or project status to stakeholders). Experience mentoring developers or guiding a technical team is a plus, as is a positive, proactive approach to teamwork and problem-solving.

• Relentless problem-solving & pace: Demonstrates exceptional speed in breaking down complex problems and removing blockers, both in code and organisational processes. Proactively identifies barriers and resolves them with urgency, ensuring rapid progress and delivery. Evidence of overcoming technical and bureaucratic obstacles to ship production AI solutions.

Desirable Criteria 

• Broad development experience – Exposure to adjacent domains like web application development, data engineering, or DevOps. This could mean you’ve built full-stack solutions or data pipelines, which helps in integrating AI solutions smoothly into broader systems (e.g. embedding an ML model output into a web interface or automating data flows for model training).

• MLOps and tooling – Familiarity with AI/ML productivity tools and best practices. For instance, experience setting up model monitoring, CI/CD for ML, or using tools that streamline AI development (from experiment tracking to automated testing of ML code). Knowledge of practices that improve developer productivity (such as using AI pair-programming assistants or scripting repetitive tasks) is beneficial.

• Community and contributions – Active interest in the AI/tech community. This might be demonstrated by contributions to open-source AI projects, publications/blog posts about AI work, or engagement in knowledge-sharing forums. A candidate who continuously learns from and contributes to the wider community will fit our culture of openness and collaboration.

It is unusual that all candidates will meet all the criteria. If your skills and experience look slightly different from what we have identified and you think you can bring value to the Applied AI Engineer role and the team, we strongly encourage you to apply. We'd love to hear from you!

Selection process details
This vacancy is using Success Profiles (opens in a new window), and will assess your Behaviours, Experience and Technical skills.
As part of the application process, you will be asked to complete a CV and personal statement. Further details around what this will entail are listed on the application form.

Please use your personal statement (in no more than 500 words) to describe how your past experience relate to the essential criteria outlined in this job description, focusing on your most recent career history.

Applications will be sifted on CV and Personal Statement.

In the event of a large number of applicants, applications will be sifted on the personal statement.

Candidates who pass the initial sift may be progressed to a full sift, or progressed straight to assessment/interview.

Feedback will only be provided if you attend an interview or assessment.
