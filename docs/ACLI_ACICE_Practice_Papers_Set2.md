# ACLI ACICE Practice Papers (Set 2 - Advanced)

## Instructions
- Harder-difficulty set with reduced thematic overlap from Set 1.

## Paper 1 (Advanced Clinical Integration)

### Question 1 (20 marks)
The following questions relate to severe T-cell immunodeficiency phenotypes and opportunistic infections.

a) List four infections strongly associated with absolute CD4+ T-cell count <50/ul. (4 marks)

b) Explain, in a mechanistic sequence, why low CD4+ counts predispose to intracellular/opportunistic pathogens. (6 marks)

c) List five baseline investigations that distinguish advanced secondary T-cell immunodeficiency from primary combined immunodeficiency. (5 marks)

d) State five immediate management priorities at first specialist review for a patient with recurrent opportunistic infection. (5 marks)

### Question 2 (20 marks)
A 7-year-old boy has severe eczema since infancy, recurrent bacterial skin infections, intermittent candidiasis, low IgG, and poor growth.

a) List three likely primary immunodeficiency differentials and one secondary immunodeficiency differential. (4 marks)

b) Name six focused history points that would most strongly separate syndromic PID from severe atopy alone. (6 marks)

c) List six first-line investigations and the rationale for each. (6 marks)

d) State four early management principles while diagnostic confirmation is pending. (4 marks)

### Question 3 (20 marks)
The following questions relate to immune phenotyping and interpretation.

a) Explain why normal total lymphocyte count does not exclude major functional immunodeficiency. (4 marks)

b) Give four examples of functional assays that may reveal pathology despite near-normal enumeration. (4 marks)

c) A patient has low switched memory B cells, impaired vaccine responses, and recurrent sinopulmonary infection. Provide a structured differential with four entities. (4 marks)

d) List four pitfalls in over-interpreting single time-point immunoglobulin measurements. (4 marks)

e) Mention four triggers for expedited genetics referral. (4 marks)

### Question 4 (20 marks)
A 16-year-old with recurrent oral candidiasis, chronic diarrhea, and severe viral warts presents for multidisciplinary review.

a) List four key immune pathways you would prioritize in your differential framework. (4 marks)

b) Propose a staged investigation pathway (at least six steps) from screening to high-specificity testing. (6 marks)

c) List five red-flag findings that increase urgency for tertiary PID center referral. (5 marks)

d) State five principles of family counseling before molecular confirmation. (5 marks)

### Question 5 (20 marks)
Laboratory quality and interpretation in complex immunology.

a) List five pre-analytical factors that may artifactually alter immunology assay interpretation. (5 marks)

b) A test has sensitivity 94%, specificity 88%, prevalence 8%. Calculate PPV and NPV. (6 marks)

c) Explain, with four points, how pre-test probability should alter your interpretation of the same lab value in two different clinics. (4 marks)

d) List five governance measures for reducing diagnostic delay in rare immunodeficiencies. (5 marks)

---

## Paper 1 Mark Scheme (Indicative)

- Q1: high-value opportunistic infections (for example CMV retinitis, disseminated MAC, cryptosporidiosis/microsporidiosis, PCP, fungal disease), coherent CD4 mechanism chain, appropriate discriminator investigations, and first-review priorities. (20)
- Q2: accepts entities such as Omenn spectrum/SCID-related disorders, WAS, IPEX, hyper-IgE pathway disorders, and secondary causes (for example HIV/immunosuppression). Marks for discriminatory history and test rationale. (20)
- Q3: emphasizes function over count; strong marks for vaccine-response assays, lymphocyte function assays, and careful interpretation limitations. (20)
- Q4: structured pathway thinking and urgency triage; points for MDT and counseling quality. (20)
- Q5:
  - a) Any 5 x 1. (5)
  - b) TP = 0.94 x 8 = 7.52
    FN = 0.06 x 8 = 0.48
    TN = 0.88 x 92 = 80.96
    FP = 0.12 x 92 = 11.04
    PPV = 7.52/(7.52 + 11.04) x 100 = 40.5%
    NPV = 80.96/(80.96 + 0.48) x 100 = 99.4% (accept 99.3 to 99.5%). (6)
  - c) Any 4 x 1. (4)
  - d) Any 5 x 1. (5)

A 19-year-old has recurrent bacterial pneumonia, chronic sinusitis, occasional severe viral infections, eczema, and low-normal IgG with poor vaccine responses.

### Question 2 (20 marks)

### Question 4 (20 marks)
Quantitative reasoning across test pathways.
Two sequential tests are used:
- Test A: sensitivity 92%, specificity 85%
- Test B: sensitivity 88%, specificity 93%
- Disease prevalence in referral cohort: 20%

a) If both tests must be positive (serial testing), estimate combined sensitivity and specificity. (6 marks)

b) Using your values, estimate PPV for serial strategy. (6 marks)

c) If either positive triggers referral (parallel strategy), estimate combined sensitivity and specificity. (4 marks)

d) Explain, in four points, when serial versus parallel strategy is preferable in immunology pathways. (4 marks)

### Question 5 (20 marks)

a) Propose five interventions to reduce time-to-diagnosis by 30% over 12 months. (5 marks)

b) Propose five interventions to reduce preventable severe infection admissions in undiagnosed PID by 20%. (5 marks)

c) Propose five multidisciplinary workflow changes to improve handoffs between immunology, respiratory, infectious diseases, and genetics. (5 marks)

d) Propose five dashboard KPIs and threshold triggers for executive review. (5 marks)

---

## Paper 5 Mark Scheme (Indicative)

- Q1-Q3: emphasis on structured differential quality, mechanism anchoring, and high-level communication ethics. (60)
- Q4:
  - Serial strategy:
    Combined sensitivity = 0.92 x 0.88 = 0.8096 (81.0%)
    Combined specificity = 1 - [(1 - 0.85) x (1 - 0.93)] = 0.9895 (99.0%)
    TP = 0.8096 x 20 = 16.192
    FP = (1 - 0.9895) x 80 = 0.84
    PPV = 16.192/(16.192 + 0.84) x 100 = 95.1%
  - Parallel strategy:
    Combined sensitivity = 1 - [(1 - 0.92) x (1 - 0.88)] = 0.9904 (99.0%)
    Combined specificity = 0.85 x 0.93 = 0.7905 (79.1%)
  - accept reasonable rounding. (20)
- Q5: reward feasibility, measurable outcomes, and operational coherence. (20)
