Here’s a clear rundown of the government-linked data tools and resources that school improvement leads in England typically use to analyse school performance and support improvement strategies I've added a table of the benefits for each department at the end too:

Official Government Data Tools
1. Analyse School Performance (ASP)
A core DfE data platform used by school leaders, governors, MATs, and local authorities. It gives detailed reports on pupil attainment, progress and outcomes for key pupil groups, with visualisations (e.g., tables, charts and scatterplots) to support evaluation and improvement planning. (GOV.UK)
Includes ability to drill down by pupil characteristic (e.g., disadvantage, SEN, EAL). (GOV.UK)
Often used alongside IDSR (Inspection Data Summary Report). (schoolgovernors.thekeysupport.com)
2. Inspection Data Summary Report (IDSR)
Produced by Ofsted and available via DfE secure systems (often accessed through ASP).
It summarises key performance data (attainment, progress, contextual info) and highlights strengths/risks — invaluable in self-evaluation and inspection preparation. (Compass Education AU)
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/school-inspection-data-summary-report-idsr-guide

3. School Comparison Tool
A DfE benchmarking resource that lets improvement leads compare their school’s performance with similar schools locally or nationally, helping to spot outliers and improvement opportunities. (Compass Education AU)
https://www.compare-school-performance.service.gov.uk/multi-academy-trust/15873/drb-ignite-multi-academy-trust/primary

4. Monitor Your School Attendance (MYSA)
A free tool to analyse and track attendance data across years and pupil groups. Useful for leads focusing on attendance strategies and improvement of persistent absence. (teaching.blog.gov.uk)
Other Government Data Sources & Measures
5. School and College Performance Measures / Official Data Releases
The DfE publishes annual performance tables and guidance on accountability measures (Progress 8, Attainment 8, KS2/KS4 outcomes), which leaders use for analysis and reporting. (GOV.UK)
This collection also includes ready reckoners and transition matrices to help leaders understand progress metrics and comparisons.

6. National Pupil Database (NPD)
Although not a front-end dashboard, the National Pupil Database is the master data source behind many official analyses and can be accessed by approved researchers/teams for deeper analytics and modelling. (Wikipedia)

Supplementary Tools Often Used Alongside Gov Data
These aren’t government systems, but are widely used by school improvement leads because they augment official data:
FFT Aspire – commercial analytics aligned to national benchmarks, often used for target setting and trend analysis. (FFT)
Perspective Lite – free dashboard for census and performance data with simple visualisations. (angelsolutions.co.uk)
MIS-Integrated Dashboards (e.g., Schools BI, Compass Pulse) – link together pupil, attendance, behaviour and attainment data for live insight. (schoolsbi.co.uk)

How These Tools Are Used in School Improvement
Self-Evaluation & SIP/SDP: Using ASP + IDSR to highlight strong/weak areas and support evidence in school improvement plans. (schoolgovernors.thekeysupport.com)
Benchmarking: Comparing trust/school performance against local & national benchmarks. (Compass Education AU)
Target Setting: Combining national accountability measures (e.g., Progress 8) with internal data trends to set realistic targets. (GOV.UK)
Thematic Analysis: Reviewing attendance, pupil groups and outcomes to tailor interventions (e.g., MYSA). (teaching.blog.gov.uk)
