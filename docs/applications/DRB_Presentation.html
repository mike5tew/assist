School Improvement

School Improvement

The Current Reality

  • Data arrives termly. By the time you see a dip, it's a gap
  • 13 schools, 13 definitions of "Pupil Premium progress"
  • SEFs are narrative-heavy, evidence-light
  • ASP and IDSR tell you where you are. Not why or what next

School Improvement

Plan & Collect

PLAN: Standardise what we measure

  • Cohort definitions (PP, SEND, EAL, mobility) consistent across 13 schools
  • Map the predictors: attendance patterns, engagement signals, transition friction

COLLECT: Make the data entry the work itself

  • Arbor feeds automated — no re-keying
  • Skills observations logged as they happen — live, not termly
  • "Tone signals" captured in 10 seconds, not written up in 10 minutes

School Improvement

Display & Act

DISPLAY: Early warning, not post-mortem

  • Attendance dip + engagement signal = intervention trigger
  • Benchmarking: your schools vs national, and vs each other
  • Power BI dashboards — real-time, role-appropriate

ACT: Targeted, evidence-based, cross-school

  • "12 students across 4 schools show the same pattern — group intervention vs individual?"
  • Cross-school best practice: "School X produced 3.2× better outcomes — here's what they did"

Method

From Flat Data to Structured Graph

459 skills. Each has 5 observable criteria, parent skills (prerequisites), offspring skills (what it unlocks)

 Number Bonds ──→ Place Value ──→ Column Addition
                                        ↑
                         Working Memory (prerequisite)

AI

Privacy by Architecture

The Federated Approach: Collective Intelligence, Complete Privacy

  1. Each school runs its own AI instance — self-contained
  2. AI builds local patterns from student interactions
  3. No personally identifiable information ever leaves the school
  4. Only anonymised patterns are shared centrally
  5. Improved models pushed back to all schools

Same architecture Google uses for keyboard prediction on your phone.
Your typing never leaves the device. The model still improves.
