## Job Purpose

Lead the development of a Trust-wide data and analytics capability that enables leaders to make informed decisions based on high quality insight. Transform the Trust from data-rich/insight-poor into an organisation that uses data strategically to drive school improvement, operational efficiency and accountability.

Ensure high-quality operational data foundations while building data intelligence capability, integrating multiple systems to provide real-time insights supporting classroom practice, school leadership and Trust-wide performance monitoring.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Data Strategy & Leadership
- Lead development and governance of Trust's Data Strategy and Performance Intelligence Framework
- Develop and implement Data & Insight Strategy aligned to educational priorities
- Establish Trust-wide standards for data governance, quality, reporting and analytics
- Promote data-driven culture across schools and central teams

### Operational Data Integrity
- Ensure high standards of data integrity across Trust MIS platforms (e.g. Arbor or equivalent)
- Support accurate and timely completion of statutory returns (census, regulatory reporting)
- Implement Trust-wide data standards and quality controls across schools
- Develop processes ensuring consistent, reliable, comparable data across Trust

### Trust Performance Analytics
- Analyse Trust-wide data: attainment, attendance, behaviour, safeguarding indicators, SEND progress
- Identify trends and provide insight to Executive Team and Trustees
- Identify patterns and emerging risks across schools, enabling earlier intervention
- Analyse relationships between attendance, behaviour, attainment and safeguarding to identify underlying causes of pupil outcomes

### Business Intelligence & Reporting
- Develop and maintain Power BI dashboards and analytics platforms
- Design automated reporting solutions reducing manual workload
- Produce performance reports for Executive leadership, Trust Board and school improvement planning

### Data Systems & Integration
- Lead development of Trust's data ecosystem (Arbor, CPOMS, helpdesk, HR platforms)
- Develop Trust-wide data architecture reducing data silos
- Support creation of centralised data repository/analytics layer for cross-school insight
- Ensure role-based access and strong data governance

### Governance & Data Quality
- Implement data governance frameworks and standards
- Ensure compliance with UK GDPR and Trust data governance policies
- Maintain audit trails and accountability processes for sensitive data

### Predictive & Diagnostic Insight
- Develop analytical approaches for early risk indicators
- Support identification of trends and predictive indicators (engagement, behaviour, attainment)
- Work with school leaders to ensure insight leads to timely interventions

### Trust-Wide Intelligence & Strategic Insight
- Provide analytical insight for Trust strategic planning, resource allocation, performance monitoring
- Cross-school analytics for Trust-level strategy and improvement
- Identify best practice patterns across schools
- Work collaboratively with Trust School Improvement Team
