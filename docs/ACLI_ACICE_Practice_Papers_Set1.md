# ACLI ACICE Practice Papers (Set 1)

## Instructions
- First paper style, answer all questions.

- Mark schemes are indicative and accept equivalent clinically correct alternatives.

## Paper 1

### Question 1 (20 marks)
The following questions relate to humoral immunodeficiency and bacterial susceptibility.

a) List three common extracellular bacteria associated with recurrent sinopulmonary infections in severe antibody deficiency states. (3 marks)

b) Give two immunological reasons why these infections are frequent in absent antibody production. (4 marks)

c) Name three baseline laboratory tests that help distinguish isolated humoral deficiency from combined immunodeficiency. (3 marks)

d) State four key long-term management principles for X-linked agammaglobulinemia (XLA). (6 marks)

e) Mention two important long-term structural complications of recurrent chest infections in antibody deficiency. (2 marks)

f) Give two examples of patient education points that reduce infection risk or delayed presentation. (2 marks)

### Question 2 (20 marks)

### Question 3 (20 marks)

a) Define opsonization and complement fixation in one line each. (4 marks)

b) Explain how IgG contributes to phagocyte-mediated killing of pyogenic organisms. (6 marks)

c) Name three phagocytic cells or cell groups involved in this process. (3 marks)

d) Give three consequences of impaired opsonization in clinical practice. (3 marks)

e) List four targeted interventions used to reduce recurrent pyogenic infection burden. (4 marks)

### Question 4 (20 marks)
A 22-year-old man with known XLA presents with productive cough, reduced exercise tolerance, and frequent winter exacerbations.

a) List three likely respiratory complications and one non-respiratory complication due to recurrent bacterial infection. (4 marks)

b) What further clinical information and investigations would you request to stage disease burden? (6 marks)

### Question 5 (20 marks)

a) Suggest five pre-analytical service changes to improve earlier detection and appropriate referrals. (5 marks)
