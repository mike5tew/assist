# HumanOS: Emotional Trigger Point (ETP) Library

## Core Philosophy: Description vs. Judgement
In the HumanOS ecosystem, an ETP is a description of the **Terrain** (the mental/emotional landscape), not a categorization of the **Student** (the explorer).
*   **Observation is not Judgement**: Recording an ETP state is like recording the weather; it tells us the current conditions so we can select the right equipment (Skills).
*   **Categorization is not Limitation**: A student's ETP profile is dynamic. It identifies where mastery (Skill) is currently being applied and where the terrain is most challenging.
*   **Sensitivity (Gain)**: We measure the "Gain" of each trigger. 
    *   *Hyper-Sensitive (High Gain)*: Signal Overload; every stimulus is "Loud."
    *   *Hypo-Sensitive (Low Gain)*: Signal Damping; stimulus is "Muffled."

---

## 1. The Internal Gates (Sensory & Survival)

### Academic Risk Tolerance (The Safety Factor)
*   **Definition**: The threshold at which the emotional cost of being wrong outweighs the potential benefit of trying.
*   **Response to Failure**: Self-Loathing/Collapse (High Gain) vs. Iteration/Learning (Low Gain).
*   **Mastery Skill**: *Resilience*, *Self-Worth decoupling*.

### Cognitive Effort Threshold (Response to Own Pain)
*   **Definition**: The point at which "Mental Strain" is perceived as "Physical Pain," triggering an energy-conservation response (Check-out).
*   **Mastery Skill**: *Metacognitive Regulation*, *Task Decomposition*.

### Response to Scarcity vs. Abundance
*   **Definition**: Fear of loss vs. drive to grow.
*   **High Scarcity Gain**: Hoarding, competition for basic resources, anxiety over "missing out."

### Voltage Sensitivity (Response to the Unknown)
*   **Definition**: Curiosity vs. Dread. The threshold between Boredom (Too low) and Anxiety (Too high).
*   **Mastery Skill**: *Emotional Regulation*, *Attention Management*.

---

## 2. The Social Gates (Interpersonal & Group)

### Social Gravity Sensitivity (Response to Being Left Out)
*   **Definition**: The degree to which the perceived opinion of the immediate peer group modulates individual behavior.
*   **High Gain**: Decisions driven by "What will they think?". High risk of "Social Short-Circuit."
*   **Mastery Skill**: *Social Intelligence*, *Personal Agency*.

### Response to Others' Pain (Empathy Gate)
*   **Definition**: The degree to which another's distress triggers an internal "Safety Check" or "Distress Signal."
*   **High Gain**: Compassion, potentially over-whelmed by others' emotions.
*   **Low Gain**: Detachment, potential for sociopathic/isolative behavior.

### Response to Praise vs. Response to Guilt
*   **Praise**: External validation. In high-integrity OS, bridges Reward (Dopamine) and Bonding (Oxytocin).
*   **Guilt**: The "Internal Governor." Prevents social friction.
*   **Mastery Skill**: *Humility*, *Social Contract Awareness*.

---

## 3. The Authority Gates (Hierarchy & Philosophical)

### Response to Authority (The "Taxes" Gate)
*   **Definition**: Duty/Social Contract (Internal Resistance) vs. Autonomy/Resistance (External Resistance).
*   **Logic**: "If I don't pay, I am a burden" vs. "This is a theft of my energy/effort."

### Response to Unfairness
*   **Definition**: Indignation/Justice vs. Resignation/Acceptance.

### Response to Ambiguity
*   **Definition**: Need for certainty/Rigidity vs. Comfort with chaos/Adaptability.

---

## 4. The Action Gates (Outputs)

### Impulse / Reflection Gap
*   **Definition**: The time-constant between the Emotional Stimulus and the Behavioral Response.
*   **Mastery Skill**: *Self-Regulation*, *Inhibition Control*.

### Agency Threshold (Autonomy vs. Compliance)
*   **Definition**: Response to external instruction (High Compliance) vs. internal drive (High Autonomy).
*   **Mastery Skill**: *Executive Function*, *Goal Setting*.
