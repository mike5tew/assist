# HumanOS: Student Sensitivity Profiles (The Descriptive Palette)

## Core Principle: The Spectrum of Calibration
These indicators are not labels; they are **Sensitivity Mappings**. Each spectrum represents the "Gain" of a student's response to specific stimuli. A teacher uses these to "tune" their delivery to keep the student in the Green Zone (Flow State).

## Usage in Diagnostic Painting: The Passive Pilot Interface

The **Passive Pilot** interface is designed for real-time, low-friction data capture in the classroom. It consists of two distinct layers:

### 1. The Profile Sliders (Static Calibration)
The **15 Sensitivity Spectrums** are represented as sliders. These are used for "Baseline Calibration" or periodic reviews. 
- **Scale**: -5 to +5.
- **Function**: Defines the student's current "HumanOS State."

### 2. The Behaviour Markers (Temporal Data)
The real-time interface allows the recording of a **set of specific behaviors (Maximum of Four)**, following the "ESP Behaviour Lite" model.
- **Temporal Nature**: These are timestamped events. They measure the **frequency and duration** of specific actions (markers) throughout a session.
- **Structure**: Two Positive Markers and Two Negative Markers.
- **Labels**: To be confirmed (currently placeholders: Positive 1/2, Negative 1/2).

---

## The Feedback Loop: Markers $\rightarrow$ Sliders
The temporal markers provide the **primary evidence** for shifting the profile sliders.
- *Logic*: If a student triggers "Task Abandonment" 5 times in 20 minutes, the system suggests a downward shift on the **Fragile — Resilient** slider.
- *The "Downwards" Rule*: Any shift to the left (downwards) on a slider requires a mandatory **Justification Note** referencing the captured temporal markers.

---

## Log Integrity & Bias Mitigation
To ensure the HumanOS remains a diagnostic tool and not a punitive one, the following protocols apply:

### 1. Objective Justification (The "Downwards" Rule)
Any time a student's profile is adjusted **downwards** (indicating lower resilience, decreased social confidence, or increased strategic deception), a mandatory **Justification Note** must be appended.

*   **Prohibited**: "Student was being lazy." (Subjective/Label)
*   **Encouraged**: "Student attempted Task X; ceased effort after 30 seconds of friction. Observed drop in postural voltage." (Objective/Evidence)

### 2. Triangulation
Profiles should ideally be "painted" by multiple observers to wash out individual teacher bias. The system flags "High-Friction Dyads" where one teacher's profile of a student differs significantly from the consensus.

### 3. Non-Parent Facing (Internal Diagnostic)
These notes are for the development of "Intervention Equipment" and context for future teachers. While not accessible via the parent portal, they must be written as "Discovery Documents"—professional, clinical, and focused on system calibration.
